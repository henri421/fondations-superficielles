Shallow foundations

Bearing, sliding, eccentricity and settlement of a shallow foundation, Eurocodes of both generations.

The recommended values of the standards are used, in the absence of a Belgian national annex. Check these values before any use on a real structure.

Footing

For a strip footing, the L line disappears and everything is per metre. Thickness 0: the self-weight of the footing and of the soil above it is already included in V.

M along B shifts the load in the direction of B, M along L in the direction of L. The two "along L" rows disappear for a strip footing.

Soil below the base
Groundwater

"Undrained" replaces φ’k and c’k with cu,k and shows the box "Water or air can reach the interface", ticked by default. That choice avoids a false refusal on a sand. There is one soil only here; the layers are used only for settlement.

Generation and approach

Switching generation keeps the data. The 1st generation offers DA1, DA2 and DA3. The 2nd generation offers (a) + (b), (c) or (d), consequence class CC2 by default, and two boxes, "Dimensions controlled" and "Count the depth factor".

σ’p is mandatory: the tool refuses rather than inventing a value. The panel is folded and empty by default.

Out of scope

This tool does not cover circular footings, an inclined base or ground, a weak layer below the base, UPL uplift, passive resistance on the front and overall stability. The national annex is not taken into account.

Without a national annex, the recommended values of the standards are used.
